# Superstore Sales Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes a Superstore sales dataset using Microsoft Excel to identify sales trends and business insights.

## Objectives

- Analyze overall sales performance
- Study monthly sales trends
- Compare sales across product categories
- Analyze regional sales performance
- Analyze sub-category sales
- Create visual charts and a dashboard
- Identify useful business insights

## Key Metrics

- Total Sales: 2,261,536.80
- Unique Orders: 4,922
- Average Sales per Order: 459.48

## Analysis Performed

### Monthly Sales Analysis
Created a Monthly Sales Trend chart to understand sales performance across January to December.

### Category Analysis
Compared sales across:
- Furniture
- Office Supplies
- Technology

### Regional Analysis
Compared sales across:
- South
- West
- Central
- East

### Sub-Category Analysis
Analyzed sales across different product sub-categories.

## Key Insights

- Technology recorded the highest sales among the three product categories.
- November recorded the highest monthly sales.
- The West region recorded the highest regional sales.
- Phones recorded the highest sales among the sub-categories.
